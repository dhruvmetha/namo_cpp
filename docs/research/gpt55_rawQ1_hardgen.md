Bottom line: the closest match to your loop is **witness-carrying generation**: make a problem by starting from a known solution/proof/path, then use search only as the verifier/training target. UED is useful for curriculum shape, but it usually **does not guarantee solvability per instance**.

Legend: **Yes** = carries a solution/proof/path. **Filter** = solvable only after solver/agent filtering. **No** = no witness.

**Five Most Relevant**
1. **DeepCubeA**: best match to “backward generate solvable states, train heuristic, reduce search.”
2. **MetaGen**: best theorem-proving analog of “synthetic hard data must carry a proof.”
3. **INT**: best difficulty-knob benchmark with proof-by-construction.
4. **ACCEL / DCD**: best UED template for mutation + replay, but needs your verifier to become reliable.
5. **Go-Explore**: best reminder to preserve rare solution paths instead of rediscovering them by rollout.
