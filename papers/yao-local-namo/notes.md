This work focuses on the keyhole problem of NAMO. Uses RL to solve for this problem, no planning, no search; Reactive policy. 

The results show that in ~11 seconds, they solve in-distribution 80%, and 55% in 14 seconds for ood. They allow the robot to push in any direction and "any number of objects".
